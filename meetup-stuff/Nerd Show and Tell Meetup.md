#### Description

​"Talk does not deliver projects"

​Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/j9DVjpguwd

Got a cool side project, robot, homelab or piece of software you are passionate about and want to show off to other nerds? Or maybe you just want to see what other nerds are talking about. Well this is the meetup for you.

At Nerd Show and Tell we give presenters up to 20 minutes to demo what's important to them.


**What count's as a Demo?**

* Personal Project
* Review some software you have used
* A tutorial you followed though and produced something from
* A tour of your Homelab
* Something that inspires you

**(Optional) Guidelines for your Demo**

* Start with a README, and read the description OUT LOUD
* Answer, What problem are you trying to solve?
* Answer, What tools are you using to solve that problem?
* Answer, What's next for this project?
* What is the happy path, user journey you dream of following?
* Ask audience for Questions
