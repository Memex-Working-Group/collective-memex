​​First, What is a [Memex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memex?utm_source=luma)?

​​A Memex is a personal knowledge interface plus search engine of you. It would contain all your personal data, contextualized, and in a annotatable linkable format.  
  
You can learn more about memex by reading [this](https://cdn.theatlantic.com/media/archives/1945/07/176-1/132407932.pdf?utm_source=luma)
