#### Definition

A [[Digital Garden]] is a digital repository of ones notes, projects, posts, photos and anything relevant to cultivating and sharing what one cares about and finds valuable.

#### Description

Digital Garden's come in all shapes and sizes. Most people cultivate them privately via apps like [[Obsidian]], [[Notion]], [[OrgMode]], or [[Anytype.io|Anytype]]. Some people publish their [[Public Knowledge Gardens|knowledge gardens as websites]].
